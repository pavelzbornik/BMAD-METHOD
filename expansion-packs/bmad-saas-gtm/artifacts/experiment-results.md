# Experiment Results — Example: QuickStart activation test

Experiment ID: QS-2025-10-A
Title: QuickStart onboarding vs Control
Hypothesis: The QuickStart onboarding flow will increase 7-day activation rate for trial users by at least 6 percentage points.

## Primary metric

- 7-day activation rate

## Summary of results

- Control: 7-day activation = 18.1% (n=10,120)
- Variant (QuickStart): 7-day activation = 25.4% (n=10,050)
- Absolute lift: +7.3pp
- Relative lift: +40.3%
- p-value (two-sided): 0.003

## Guardrails

- Error rate: no significant change (0.12% → 0.14%)
- Support tickets: +4% (within threshold)

## Recommendation

- Promote QuickStart to 20% of eligible users while monitoring 14-day retention.
- Plan follow-up experiments to optimize copy and drop-off points within the flow.
